## WCAG Notes

- PASS rows are pre-verified for AA body text (bg vs text >= 4.5:1) and accent legibility (accent vs accent-contrast >= 4.5:1, accent vs bg >= 3:1).
- WARN rows pass body text but fail an accent rule: shift accent lightness/saturation, never swap hue family. Re-run scripts/contrast.py after any change.
- FAIL rows must not ship; either fix before selection or exclude the palette.
- Dark mode re-verification required: contrast is not symmetric across modes; re-run ratio checks on dark tokens.

## Lila Rule (Banned Accent Families)

Never: AI purple/violet glow hexes (the three standard violet shades listed in detect.sh check 05), cyan-to-magenta gradients, neon-on-dark glow. If a palette suggests purple or cyan, replace it.

## Warm-Beige Families (Banned for Premium-Consumer)

For cookware, wellness, artisan luxury, heritage craft, DTC home goods: never use the warm-craft family. Banned background family: 7 cream/beige hexes in the #E8..#FB warm-beige band (see detect.sh check 02 for exact hexes). Banned accent family: 6 brass/clay/oxblood/ochre hexes (see detect.sh check 03).

Worked premium alternatives: Bone + Silver-Grey; Black + Tan; Cobalt + Cream; Terracotta + Olive; Off-White + Off-Black.
